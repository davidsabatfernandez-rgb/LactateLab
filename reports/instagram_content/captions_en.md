## Carousel 6: How to read a lactate curve in 60 seconds

Save this post. You'll use it in every analysis.

Shape matters: convex = trained athlete, concave = needs base. Red flags: high baseline lactate, shifted curve, sudden jumps.

## Carousel 13: Norwegian Method: what works and what doesn't

The Norwegian Method isn't magic. It's precision.

Lactate-guided intervals: maintain 2-3 mmol/L. It works because it prevents exceeding LT2 and maximizes time in the productive zone.

BUT: it only works for athletes with good aerobic base (high LT1, ratio > 0.85). If your LT2 is at 2.5 mmol, working at 2-3 mmol is supra-threshold, not threshold development.

The mistake: applying the method without knowing your real thresholds.

First measure. Then decide the method. peakaerobic.com
