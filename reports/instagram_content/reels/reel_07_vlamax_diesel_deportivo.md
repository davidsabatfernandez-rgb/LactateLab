**Visual:** Dos iconos de motor confrontados.

## Caption (Espanol)
VLamax = velocidad maxima de produccion de lactato.

VLamax BAJA (< 0.3 mmol/L/s):
- Tu motor funciona con grasa
- Produces poco lactato incluso a alta intensidad
- Ideal para larga distancia: Ironman, maraton, ultras

VLamax ALTA (> 0.5 mmol/L/s):
- Tu motor funciona con glucogeno
- Gran explosion de potencia
- Ideal para sprints, 5K, pista

El truco: la VLamax se puede ENTRENAR. Con bloques aerobicos (Olbrecht AEC) la reduces. Con bloques anaerobicos (ANC) la subes.

PeakAerobic estima tu VLamax desde la forma de tu curva de lactato.

## Caption (English)
VLamax = maximum rate of lactate production.

LOW VLamax (< 0.3 mmol/L/s):
- Your engine runs on fat
- Little lactate even at high intensity
- Ideal for long distance: Ironman, marathon, ultras

HIGH VLamax (> 0.5 mmol/L/s):
- Your engine runs on glycogen
- Great power explosion
- Ideal for sprints, 5K, track

The trick: VLamax is TRAINABLE. With aerobic blocks (Olbrecht AEC) you reduce it. With anaerobic blocks (ANC) you increase it.

PeakAerobic estimates your VLamax from your lactate curve shape.
