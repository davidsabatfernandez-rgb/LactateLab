## Concepto

## Caption (Espanol)
LT1 — el umbral aerobico — marca el inicio de la acumulacion de lactato sobre la linea base (Skinner & McLellan, 1980; Faude et al., 2009).

La investigacion sugiere que la mayor parte del volumen de entrenamiento de los atletas de resistencia de elite se realiza por debajo de este punto (Seiler, 2010). Es la base fisiologica sobre la que se construyen todas las demas capacidades.

Sin embargo, muchos programas de entrenamiento ni siquiera lo miden. Se enfocan solo en el LT2 (umbral anaerobico) o en la FC maxima.

Conocer tu LT1 individual te permite entrenar la base aerobica con precision.

Refs:
- Skinner & McLellan (1980) — Two thresholds concept
- Faude et al. (2009) Sports Med — Lactate threshold review
- Seiler (2010) Sports Med — Training intensity distribution

## Caption (English)
LT1 — the aerobic threshold — marks the onset of lactate accumulation above baseline (Skinner & McLellan, 1980; Faude et al., 2009).

Research suggests that the majority of training volume in elite endurance athletes is performed below this point (Seiler, 2010). It is the physiological foundation upon which all other capacities are built.

Yet many training programs don't even measure it. They focus solely on LT2 (anaerobic threshold) or max heart rate.

Knowing your individual LT1 allows you to train the aerobic base with precision.

Refs:
- Skinner & McLellan (1980) — Two thresholds concept
- Faude et al. (2009) Sports Med — Lactate threshold review
- Seiler (2010) Sports Med — Training intensity distribution
