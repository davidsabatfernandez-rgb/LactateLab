# Reel 09: Los 4 bloques de Olbrecht — periodizacion por sistemas energeticos

## Concepto
Presentar los 4 tipos de bloque de entrenamiento de Olbrecht (AEC, AEP, ANC, ANP) como un framework de periodizacion por bloques que focaliza el estimulo en un sistema energetico por fase. Basado en el modelo de Olbrecht (2000) y el metaanalisis de Issurin (2010) sobre block periodization.

## Caption (Espanol)
La periodizacion por bloques concentra el estimulo en un sistema energetico por fase, en lugar de entrenar todo a la vez. Jan Olbrecht propuso 4 tipos de bloque (AEC, AEP, ANC, ANP) basados en la interaccion entre capacidad aerobica y via glucolitica. Cada bloque responde a un perfil metabolico concreto, determinado por test de lactato.

"Block periodization allows for a concentrated training load directed at a minimal number of motor and technical abilities" — Issurin (2010), Sports Med.

Refs:
- Olbrecht, J. (2000). The Science of Winning. Luton.
- Issurin, V.B. (2010). New horizons for the methodology and physiology of training periodization. Sports Medicine, 40(3), 189-206.

## Caption (English)
Block periodization concentrates training stimulus on one energy system per phase, rather than training everything simultaneously. Jan Olbrecht proposed 4 block types (AEC, AEP, ANC, ANP) based on the interaction between aerobic capacity and the glycolytic pathway. Each block addresses a specific metabolic profile determined by lactate testing.

Refs:
- Olbrecht, J. (2000). The Science of Winning. Luton.
- Issurin, V.B. (2010). Sports Medicine, 40(3), 189-206.

## Scientific rigor notes
- Olbrecht (2000) is the primary source for the 4-block model (AEC/AEP/ANC/ANP)
- Issurin (2010) provides the meta-evidence for block periodization superiority in trained athletes
- All claims are descriptive of the framework, not causal claims about outcomes
- "Desplazar la curva hacia la derecha" is an established metaphor for improved lactate kinetics at submaximal intensities
