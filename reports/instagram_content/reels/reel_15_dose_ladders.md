## Caption (Espanol)
Tu atleta hizo 4x6' a ritmo de LT2 y lo clavo. Que prescribes ahora?

La mayoria de coaches adivinan: "bueno, subimos un poco".

En PeakAerobic usamos dose ladders: escaleras de progresion con 6-8 peldanos predefinidos para cada familia de sesion.

Ejemplo (LT2 Cruise Intervals):
1. 3x5' @ LT2
2. 4x5' @ LT2
3. 3x8' @ LT2
4. 4x6' @ LT2
5. 4x8' @ LT2
6. 3x12' @ LT2

El sistema lee la respuesta del atleta y auto-selecciona el siguiente peldano:
- Respuesta positiva + bloque reciente = subir
- Respuesta neutra = mantener
- Respuesta negativa = bajar

Sin adivinar. Sin sobreentrenar. Progresion sistematica.

## Caption (English)
Your athlete nailed 4x6' at LT2 pace. What do you prescribe now?

Most coaches guess: "well, let's go up a bit."

In PeakAerobic we use dose ladders: progression ladders with 6-8 predefined steps for each session family.

Example (LT2 Cruise Intervals):
1. 3x5' @ LT2
2. 4x5' @ LT2
3. 3x8' @ LT2
4. 4x6' @ LT2
5. 4x8' @ LT2
6. 3x12' @ LT2

The system reads the athlete's response and auto-selects the next step:
- Positive response + recent block = step up
- Neutral response = maintain
- Negative response = step down

No guessing. No overtraining. Systematic progression.
