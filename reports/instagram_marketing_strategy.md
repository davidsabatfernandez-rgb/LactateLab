# PeakAerobic Instagram Content Strategy
## Comprehensive Marketing Plan -- March 2026

---

## 1. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: "The Science Behind the Curve" (Education)
**Description:** Break down lactate physiology, threshold detection, VO2max, VLamax, and periodization concepts into digestible visual content. Position PeakAerobic as the authoritative voice on lactate-based training.

**Why it works:** The Norwegian Method trend has made lactate testing mainstream. Coaches and athletes are hungry for real science, not oversimplified "Zone 2" content. This pillar captures search intent and establishes credibility.

**Best format:** Carousel (educational slides), Reels (visual explainers)

**5 post ideas:**
1. **"Your lactate curve is lying to you"** -- Carousel showing how a single-point 4mmol test misses the real LT2 vs. a full curve analysis in PeakAerobic
2. **"LT1 vs LT2: Why most coaches only train one"** -- Reel with whiteboard-style animation overlaid on running footage
3. **"What VLamax actually tells you (that VO2max can't)"** -- Carousel with Olbrecht framework simplified into 6 slides
4. **"The 3 mistakes coaches make reading lactate curves"** -- Reel showing common misinterpretations with real (anonymized) data
5. **"Why your athlete's LT2 at 4mmol is wrong"** -- Static infographic comparing fixed 4mmol vs. individualized ModDmax detection

---

### Pillar 2: "Coach's Toolbox" (Product Demos & Workflows)
**Description:** Screen recordings, walkthroughs, and workflow demos showing PeakAerobic in action. Not hard sells -- show the tool solving real coaching problems.

**Why it works:** Coaches buy tools they can visualize using. INSCYD does this well but their content is polished and corporate. PeakAerobic can win with raw, authentic "here's how I actually use it" content.

**Best format:** Reels (screen recordings with voiceover), Stories (quick tips)

**5 post ideas:**
1. **"I analyzed a lactate test in 30 seconds -- here's how"** -- Reel: phone screen recording uploading data, seeing curves auto-generate
2. **"From lactate test to Garmin workout in 3 clicks"** -- Reel showing the full pipeline: test -> thresholds -> mesocycle -> push to Garmin
3. **"How PeakAerobic detects your athlete's real LT1"** -- Reel zooming into the curve with annotations appearing in real-time
4. **"My Monday morning coaching workflow"** -- Story series: check athlete data, review lactate evolution, adjust plan
5. **"The one feature that changed how I coach"** -- Reel about dynamic threshold tracking across multiple tests over time

---

### Pillar 3: "Before & After the Data" (Transformations & Case Studies)
**Description:** Show real athlete progressions -- lactate curves shifting right over weeks/months, pace improvements at same lactate levels, threshold evolution.

**Why it works:** Transformation content is Instagram's highest-performing format. But instead of body transformations, these are PERFORMANCE transformations. Novel, shareable, and deeply credible.

**Best format:** Carousel (before/after curves side by side), Reels (timelapse of evolution)

**5 post ideas:**
1. **"8 weeks. Same lactate. 25 seconds faster per km."** -- Carousel: curve overlay from test 1 vs test 3
2. **"This triathlete's LT2 shifted 15 watts in one mesocycle"** -- Reel with dramatic reveal of the second curve
3. **"What 12 weeks of Olbrecht periodization looks like in data"** -- Carousel walking through the physiological changes block by block
4. **"From 4:45/km to 4:15/km at threshold -- the data doesn't lie"** -- Static post with curve comparison and training summary
5. **"The curve that made me change this athlete's entire plan"** -- Reel: storytelling format, showing the unexpected data point that triggered a block change

---

### Pillar 4: "Coach Talk" (Community & Opinion)
**Description:** Hot takes, debates, and thought leadership on training philosophy. Engage the coaching community in conversation.

**Why it works:** Controversy and opinion drive comments. Comments drive reach. The endurance coaching community loves debating methods. This pillar turns followers into a community.

**Best format:** Static (text-heavy graphics), Reels (talking head), Stories (polls)

**5 post ideas:**
1. **"Unpopular opinion: Most coaches overtrain their athletes at LT2"** -- Static text post with bold typography
2. **"Should age-groupers do double threshold days?"** -- Story poll + follow-up Reel with David's take
3. **"The Norwegian Method is great. But it's not for everyone."** -- Reel: talking head with data overlay showing when it fails
4. **"Zone 2 is overhyped. Here's what the lactate actually says."** -- Carousel debunking common Zone 2 misconceptions
5. **"What I wish I knew before I started coaching with lactate"** -- Reel: personal story format, authentic and reflective

**Description:** Concrete training advice derived from lactate science. Workouts, session design, recovery protocols -- all tied back to what the data says.

**Why it works:** This is the broadest-reach pillar. Athletes who may never buy PeakAerobic still follow for the training advice, building top-of-funnel awareness. Coaches share these with their athletes.

**Best format:** Carousels (workout breakdowns), Reels (session demos), Stories (quick tips)
