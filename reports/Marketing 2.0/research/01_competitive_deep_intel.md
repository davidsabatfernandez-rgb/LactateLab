# Competitive Deep Intelligence Report — PeakAerobic

**Date:** 2026-03-17
**Analyst:** PeakAerobic Competitive Intelligence
**Scope:** 9 competitors across lactate analysis, training planning, and AI coaching

---

## Executive Summary

PeakAerobic occupies a unique position: **the only free tool that combines lactate test analysis, VLamax estimation, Olbrecht-based periodization, and training zone calculation**. No single competitor covers all four pillars. The market is split between expensive metabolic profiling tools (INSCYD, Winlactat), generic planning platforms (TrainingPeaks, Intervals.icu), AI plan generators (AI Endurance, Athletica, JOIN), and legacy desktop software (WKO5, Winlactat). PeakAerobic's free pricing and integrated physiology-to-planning pipeline is a defensible wedge.

## 1. INSCYD — The Main Rival

### Pricing Model

| Tier | Cost | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| Starter | From **EUR 49/month** | Unlimited athletes; pricing scales with testing volume |
| Custom | Quote-based | Tailored to business model; requires demo call |

| INSCYD College | Included | E-learning courses, certification for partners |

INSCYD does **not** publish transparent pricing. They force a sales call ("Book a Demo") to discuss packages. This is a deliberate opacity strategy to maximize per-coach revenue.

### Core Features

- **VLamax** (glycolytic power) — their flagship metric, "the only scientifically validated measurement"
- **VO2max** estimation from field tests (power-based PPD protocol)
- **FatMax / CarbMax** — fuel utilization curves
- **Lactate curve modeling** from blood lactate or power-only tests
- **Performance Projection** — "what if VO2max increases by 3%?" virtual athlete simulator
- **Training zones** based on metabolic profile (not just FTP/LTHR)
- **Remote testing** — athlete does 4 intervals on smart trainer, coach analyzes remotely
- Integration with TrainingPeaks, WKO, Today's Plan

### What INSCYD Does That PeakAerobic Doesn't

- **Power-only testing (PPD)** — no blood required, estimates VLamax from max efforts
- **FatMax / CarbMax curves** — fuel utilization modeling
- **Performance Projection** — virtual athlete "what if" scenarios
- **Automated indoor testing** via Velocity integration
- **Certification ecosystem** — INSCYD College creates coach lock-in
- **Marketing network** reaching 80,000+ athletes/month seeking certified coaches
- **Brand credibility** — used by Jumbo-Visma, Alpecin-Fenix, Movistar, Lionel Sanders

### What PeakAerobic Does That INSCYD Doesn't

- **Free** — vs EUR 49+/month
- **Olbrecht-based periodization engine** — INSCYD stops at profiling, doesn't prescribe mesocycles
- **Dynamic threshold tracking** over time with multi-session regression
- **Multiple LT detection methods** (ModDmax, baseline_rise, sustained_increase) with aggregation
- **Real thresholds** (LT1/LT2 by curve shape) with confidence gating
- **Workout library** with dose ladders and coach-specific templates
- **Calendar-based planning** with microcycle validation
- **No sales call required** — instant access

### Marketing Messaging

INSCYD uses aspirational, authority-based language:
- *"360-degree Metabolic Profile"*
- *"The secret weapon of elite coaches"*
- *"Make physiological testing accessible, profitable, scalable, and confident"*
- *"Turn conventional lactate tests into 360-degree Athletic Performance Assessment"*
- Emphasis on **business growth** for coaches: "Boost athlete retention," "Grow your coaching business"

### User Complaints / Reviews

- **Opaque pricing** — coaches complain about not knowing costs upfront
- **Steep learning curve** — requires certification/education to interpret results
- **Gated access** — must attend demo/sales call before purchasing
- **Transparency concern** — sales page omits mention of blood sampling despite FAQ discussing it
- **Limited to profiling** — no planning, no calendar, no workout prescription

> *"INSCYD is essentially a profiling tool. Once you have the numbers, you're on your own to figure out what to do with them."* — Forum user, TrainerRoad

### Target Audience

Professional coaches, sports science labs, national federations, World Tour teams. Increasingly targeting "ambitious age-groupers" willing to pay EUR 150-300 per test.

### Content Strategy

Heavy investment in educational content: webinars, INSCYD College courses, blog posts explaining VLamax/VO2max. Positions itself as the "scientific authority." Strong presence at coaching conferences. Instagram focuses on pro athlete testimonials and data visualizations.

## Gaps PeakAerobic Can Exploit

### GAP 1: "INSCYD for Free" Positioning
INSCYD charges EUR 49+/month and requires a sales call. PeakAerobic offers lactate analysis + VLamax estimation for free. **Message: "Why pay EUR 49/month for metabolic profiling when PeakAerobic gives you VLamax, LT1, LT2, and VO2max estimation from your lactate tests — for free?"**

### GAP 2: Profiling-to-Planning Bridge
INSCYD profiles but doesn't plan. TrainingPeaks plans but doesn't profile. **PeakAerobic is the only tool that goes from lactate test to training block prescription in one platform.** No competitor connects physiological gaps to Olbrecht-based mesocycle selection.

### GAP 3: Coach-Friendly Pricing
TrainingPeaks charges $4.50-9/athlete/month. A coach with 30 athletes pays $135-270/month just for the platform. PeakAerobic is free. **Message: "Stop paying per-athlete fees. PeakAerobic gives your coaching business full physiological analysis and planning for $0."**

### GAP 4: Transparent Science vs. Black-Box AI
AI Endurance, Athletica, and JOIN use opaque AI algorithms. Athletes and coaches can't see why a workout was prescribed. PeakAerobic shows **block candidates with scores, physiological rationale, dose ladders, and reliability warnings.** The coach stays in control.

### GAP 5: Modern UX for Lactate Analysis
Winlactat has a 2005-era Windows-only interface at EUR 98/month. Ergonizer is similarly dated. **PeakAerobic is the modern, web-based, free alternative** for coaches who do lactate testing but don't want desktop software from the previous decade.

### GAP 6: No Certification Required
INSCYD requires (or strongly encourages) certification through INSCYD College before coaches can effectively use the tool. PeakAerobic should position itself as **immediately usable** — upload your test, get your thresholds, see your training recommendations. No gatekeeping.

### GAP 7: Multi-Session Longitudinal Tracking
Most competitors analyze individual tests in isolation. PeakAerobic's **dynamic threshold engine** tracks trends across multiple sessions with outlier detection, multi-bracket interpolation, and physiological anchoring. This is unique in the free tier.

### GAP 8: Olbrecht Is Underserved
Jan Olbrecht's periodization methodology (Science of Winning) is referenced by Athletica and others but **not implemented as a structured engine** anywhere except PeakAerobic. The 6 Olbrecht blocks (AEC, THR, AEP, ANC, ANP, COMP) with capacity profiling and VLamax-aware block selection is a genuine differentiator.

### GAP 9: Intervals.icu Users Want Metabolic Features
The Intervals.icu forum has multiple feature requests for VLamax estimation, lactate threshold detection, and INSCYD-like modeling — all unfulfilled. These users are **data-literate, price-sensitive, and already using a free tool.** They are the perfect PeakAerobic early adopters.

### GAP 10: TrainingPeaks Billing Frustration
TrainingPeaks has significant billing complaints on Trustpilot and forums. Coaches who feel nickel-and-dimed by per-athlete fees are primed to hear about a free alternative that adds physiological depth TrainingPeaks lacks.
