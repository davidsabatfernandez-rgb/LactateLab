## 3. Competitor SEO Positioning

### 3.1 INSCYD (inscyd.com)

**Content strategy:** Extremely aggressive SEO. They write "vs" pages (INSCYD vs Mesics, vs TrainingPeaks) to OWN comparison queries. They also write definitive guides on topics they want to rank for (VLamax, LT1, 4mmol myth, lactate curve interpretation).

**Key pages that rank well:**
- "VLamax: Elite Coaches Secret Weapon" — ranks for VLamax queries
- "Does the anaerobic threshold really occur at 4 mmol/l?" — ranks for 4mmol skepticism queries
- "How To Interpret A Shift In Lactate Curves" — ranks for interpretation queries
- "Mesics vs INSCYD" — controls comparison narrative
- "Lactate Testing for Sports: Ultimate Guide" — ranks for general lactate testing queries

**Pricing:** EUR 49/month minimum. Not accessible to solo coaches or amateurs.

**Weakness:** They ONLY sell to coaches/labs (not athletes directly). No free tier. No open data. Their content strategy is "gatekeeper" — generate demand, then require demo + subscription.

**PeakAerobic opportunity:** Be the "open" alternative. Free tool, transparent methodology, educational content that doesn't require a paywall.

### 3.2 TrainingPeaks (trainingpeaks.com)

**Lactate content:** 8+ blog posts about lactate, primarily by Joe Friel. Educational, well-written, but surface-level.

**Key pages:**
- "What is Lactate and Lactate Threshold"
- "A Coach's Guide to Lactate Testing"
- "Joe Friel's Quick Guide to Setting Zones"

**Weakness:** TrainingPeaks is a training LOG, not a lactate analysis tool. Their content is informational but has no tool CTA. They don't analyze lactate data — they just talk about it.

**PeakAerobic opportunity:** TrainingPeaks content generates demand for lactate analysis but doesn't fulfill it. PeakAerobic could be the natural next step: "Analyzed your lactate test? Now plan your training in TrainingPeaks" or vice versa.

### 3.3 Intervals.icu

**Lactate content:** Forum discussions only. No official blog content about lactate.

**Weakness:** Intervals.icu is a data platform, not a lactate analysis tool. Their community WANTS lactate features but the platform doesn't provide them natively.

**PeakAerobic opportunity:** The Intervals.icu community is a perfect audience. Forum posts, integrations, and "how to use your Intervals.icu data with PeakAerobic" content could drive organic discovery.

## 6. Technical SEO Recommendations

1. **Schema markup:** Add `SoftwareApplication` schema to the app's main page. Include `offers: {price: "0"}` for "free" signals.
2. **Tool landing pages:** Each tool/calculator should have its own URL with targeted H1, meta description, and embedded tool (not behind login).
3. **Internal linking:** Every blog post should link to the relevant tool page with contextual anchor text.
4. **Title tag formulas:**
   - Tool pages: "[Benefit] | [Tool Type] | PeakAerobic" — e.g., "Free Lactate Curve Analysis | Online Calculator | PeakAerobic"
   - Blog posts: "[Question/Provocation] — [Context]" — e.g., "Why 4mmol Is Not Your Lactate Threshold — The Science"
5. **Meta descriptions:** Include "free," "no sign-up," "instant results" for tool pages.
6. **Alt text on images:** Describe curve shapes specifically (e.g., "lactate curve showing right-shift after 8 weeks of base training").

## 8. Key Takeaways

1. **The biggest opportunity is "INSCYD alternative free"** — high commercial intent, zero independent content, PeakAerobic is the answer.
2. **"Olbrecht periodization software" has ZERO competition** — PeakAerobic is literally the only implementation in software.
3. **Tool pages rank for transactional queries** — PeakAerobic needs dedicated, SEO-optimized landing pages for each tool (analyzer, zones calculator, race predictor).
4. **The "4mmol is wrong" narrative is powerful** — INSCYD uses it effectively. PeakAerobic should write its own version with real examples.
5. **ModDmax has zero practitioner content** — 100% academic papers. First coach-friendly explainer wins the query permanently.
6. **Existing free tools are weak** — Lactate-OR, LaChart, ExPhysLab are basic. PeakAerobic's multi-method analysis + athlete history + periodization + Garmin push is categorically different.
7. **Blog content is the main format that ranks** — for most lactate-related queries, blog posts outrank tools. PeakAerobic needs content + tools, not tools alone.
8. **The DIY lactate test audience is large and underserved** — many guides on protocol, nobody closes the loop to analysis. This is a perfect top-of-funnel.
