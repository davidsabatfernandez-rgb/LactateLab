## 1.2 Positioning Statement

> **For endurance coaches and athletes who perform lactate tests**, PeakAerobic is the only platform that automatically detects physiological thresholds (LT1/LT2), tracks their evolution over time, and generates science-backed training plans — **unlike** INSCYD (complex, expensive, cycling-centric), Mesics/WinLactat (desktop-only, no planning), or generic platforms like TrainingPeaks (no lactate analysis at all).

### Competitive Differentiators

| Feature | PeakAerobic | INSCYD | Mesics/WinLactat | TrainingPeaks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic LT1/LT2 detection | Yes (3 methods + aggregation) | Manual model fitting | Semi-auto | No |
| Longitudinal threshold tracking | Yes (dynamic engine) | No | Limited | No |
| Multi-discipline (run/bike/swim) | Yes | Cycling-focused | Yes | N/A |
| Training plan generation | Yes (Olbrecht-based) | No | No | Third-party |
| VO2max / VLamax estimation | Yes (Swain + Mader) | Yes (metabolic model) | Limited | No |
| Garmin workout push | Yes | No | No | Yes |
| Price | EUR 15-80/mo | EUR 50-200/mo | EUR 300+ license | EUR 10-30/mo |
| Web-based | Yes | Yes | Desktop | Yes |

## 1.3 Content Pillars

All content maps to one of these five pillars:

### Pillar 1: "La ciencia, simple" (Science Made Simple)
- Lactate physiology explained visually
- LT1/LT2 concepts, VO2max, VLamax, fat oxidation
- Myth-busting (Zone 2 myths, "fat-burning zone," etc.)
- **Ratio:** 30% of total content
- **Format:** Carousels (ES/EN), Reels with whiteboard/screen animations

### Pillar 2: "Resultados reales" (Real Results)
- Before/after threshold shifts from real athletes
- Case studies: "How Coach X improved 12 athletes' LT2 in one mesocycle"
- Race results tied back to lactate data
- **Ratio:** 25% of total content
- **Format:** Reels (transformation stories), carousels (data walkthrough)

### Pillar 3: "Dentro de PeakAerobic" (Inside the Platform)
- Product demos, feature walkthroughs, new feature announcements
- Screen recordings: uploading a test, reading the analysis, creating a plan
- Tips and tricks for power users
- **Ratio:** 20% of total content
- **Format:** Reels (quick tips), Stories (feature drops), carousels (step-by-step)

### Pillar 4: "El entrenador moderno" (The Modern Coach)
- Coaching methodology: Olbrecht blocks, periodization, dose-response
- How to use lactate data in coaching decisions
- Business of coaching: differentiation, pricing, client retention
- **Ratio:** 15% of total content
- **Format:** Carousels (coaching frameworks), Reels (talking head), YouTube Shorts

### Pillar 5: "Comunidad" (Community)
- User spotlights, coach testimonials, Q&A
- Polls, quizzes, challenges
- Behind the scenes of product development
- **Ratio:** 10% of total content
- **Format:** Stories, interactive posts, Lives

## 1.4 Funnel Stages

```
AWARENESS          INTEREST           TRIAL              CONVERSION
(Reach new         (Educate &         (Free trial /      (Paid subscription)
 audiences)         nurture)           lead magnet)

Reels/Shorts  -->  Carousels    -->  Landing page  -->  Onboarding email
TikTok virals      Email nurture     Free test upload    In-app upgrade
Collabs/pods       Blog/SEO          Zone calculator     Coach plan upsell
Paid reach ads     Retargeting       PDF guide           Annual discount
```

### Stage 1: Awareness
- **Goal:** Reach 50K new accounts/month within 90 days
- **Tactics:** Reels optimized for Explore (hook in <3s), TikTok duets with coaches, YouTube Shorts (search-optimized: "what is LT2," "lactate test explained")
- **KPI:** Reach, impressions, profile visits, follower growth rate

### Stage 2: Interest
- **Goal:** Convert 5-8% of reached accounts into engaged followers
- **Tactics:** Educational carousels (save-worthy), email capture via lead magnets, DM automation (comment "LACTATE" to get the free guide)
- **KPI:** Saves, shares, DM conversations, email list growth

### Stage 3: Trial
- **Goal:** Convert 15-25% of email list into free trial users
- **Tactics:** Free test upload (no credit card), zone calculator tool, webinar with live demo
- **KPI:** Trial signups, first-test-uploaded rate, activation rate

### Stage 4: Conversion
- **Goal:** Convert 20-30% of trial users into paid subscribers
- **Tactics:** In-app upgrade prompts after "aha moment" (seeing first threshold analysis), limited-time coach plan offers, annual plan discount (2 months free)
- **KPI:** Trial-to-paid rate, MRR, churn rate, LTV

## 3.3 Comment Engagement Playbook

### Response Time Target
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours during peak hours (7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 6-9 PM CET)
- Pin the best question/comment to encourage more discussion

### Comment Engagement Tactics

**The "Value Add" reply:** When someone asks a question, answer it thoroughly and end with a follow-up question.
> User: "Is this the same as an FTP test?"
> Reply: "@user Great question! FTP is a proxy for your lactate threshold but it can drift 5-15% from your real LT2. A lactate test measures the actual metabolic shift. Are you using FTP for your zone 2 training right now?"

**The "Redirect to DM":** For complex questions that could lead to a trial signup.
> Reply: "@user That's a deeper question — DM us and we'll show you how PeakAerobic would analyze your specific case."

**The "Community Tag":** When someone shares a result or story.
> Reply: "@user That's an incredible LT2 shift! Would you be open to us featuring your journey? Tag us in your next test post."

**The "Controversy Spark":** Leave thoughtful comments on competitor posts or popular fitness posts.
> On a post about Zone 2: "Most Zone 2 prescriptions are based on 220-age formulas. A single lactate test would save months of wasted training."

### Proactive Comment Strategy
- Comment on 15-20 relevant posts daily from hashtags: #LactateTest #TriathlonTraining #EnduranceCoaching
- Focus on posts from coaches and age-groupers (not other brands)
- Always provide value, never spam
