## 10. Key Takeaways

1. **VLamax is the biggest gap.** INSCYD owns this concept but charges premium prices. A free VLamax calculator and educational content would capture thousands of monthly searches with near-zero competition.

2. **Spanish market is underserved.** Technical endurance content in Spanish has almost no competition. "Test de lactato", "curva de lactato", "software entrenadores" are wide open.

3. **Norwegian method + zone 2 are traffic magnets.** These trending topics drive massive volume. The unique PeakAerobic angle: show real lactate data behind these methods.

4. **Continuous lactate monitors are the next wave.** Content about CLMs is growing +200% YoY. Position PeakAerobic as the analysis platform for when CLMs go mainstream.

5. **"Alternative to INSCYD" is a gift keyword.** High intent, low competition, exact product-market fit. Should be a Day 1 priority.

6. **Equipment integration content converts.** "Lactate Pro 2 software", "Lactate Scout app" — device owners actively searching for analysis tools. PeakAerobic solves this.
