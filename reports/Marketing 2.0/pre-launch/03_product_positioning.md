## 1. POSITIONING STATEMENT

> For **endurance coaches** who need to turn lactate test data into scientifically grounded training prescriptions, **PeakAerobic** is the **free web-based training intelligence platform** that analyzes lactate curves with three validated methods, estimates VO2max and VLamax, and generates periodized training blocks based on Olbrecht's methodology. ## 5. BRAND VOICE GUIDELINES

### Tone
**Authoritative but approachable. Scientific but not academic.**

PeakAerobic sounds like a well-read coach who can cite the paper and explain the concept over coffee. Not a professor reading a slide deck. Not a startup founder saying "disrupt."

### Do's

- **Cite papers.** "ModDmax follows Bishop 1998" is better than "our advanced algorithm."
- **Use specific numbers.** "Confidence score of 0.82" is better than "high confidence."
- **Show the math.** "LT1 detected at baseline + 0.5 mmol (Faude 2009)" is better than "we identify your aerobic threshold."
- **Be transparent about limitations.** "With fewer than 5 data points, confidence drops below 0.6 and we flag the result" is better than silence.
- **Explain jargon when you use it.** "VLamax (your maximum glycolytic rate -- how fast you burn sugar)" on first use.
- **Compare features, not brands.** "Unlike tools that use a single detection method..." not "Unlike Brand X which gets it wrong."
- **Acknowledge the user's intelligence.** Assume they can understand the science if you explain it clearly.
- **Use active voice.** "PeakAerobic analyzes your test" not "Your test is analyzed by PeakAerobic."

### Don'ts

- **No hype.** Never say "revolutionary," "game-changing," "world's best," or "cutting-edge."
- **No vague claims.** Never say "powered by advanced AI" or "proprietary algorithm." If it's a regression, call it a regression.
- **No competitor attacks.** Never say "INSCYD is overpriced garbage." Say "tools with similar features typically cost EUR 49+/month."
- **No unexplained jargon.** Never drop "VLamax" or "ModDmax" without context on first use.
- **No false precision.** Never say "your VO2max is 58.3 mL/kg/min" without noting it's an estimate with a confidence range.
- **No promises about performance.** Never say "PeakAerobic will make you faster." Say "PeakAerobic helps you understand your physiology and train more effectively."
- **No startup cliches.** Never say "disrupt," "10x," "unlock your potential," or "data-driven."

### Example Phrases That Sound Like PeakAerobic

- "Three methods. One confidence score. You decide."
- "LT2 detected at 4:12/km (confidence 0.87, agreement across all three methods)."
- "Your aerobic capacity is strong, but the LT1-LT2 gap suggests room for threshold development -- here's a 4-week block for that."
- "ModDmax was developed by Bishop et al. (1998) specifically because standard Dmax fails on well-trained athletes. If your curve is convex, this matters."
- "We show our work. Every threshold, every method, every confidence score is visible."
- "Free means free. No trial period. No premium tier hiding the features you need."

### Example Phrases That DO NOT Sound Like PeakAerobic

- "Unlock your ultimate athletic potential with our cutting-edge AI platform!"
- "PeakAerobic's proprietary algorithm crushes the competition."
- "Join the revolution in sports science!"

## 7. PRODUCT NARRATIVE

### The Problem

Every endurance coach who takes physiology seriously hits the same wall.

You do a lactate test. You get numbers. You open Excel, plot the curve, squint at it, and try to figure out where the thresholds are. If you are careful, you run a Dmax calculation -- but Dmax was designed in the 1990s and fails on well-trained athletes whose curves are convex instead of exponential. You try the fixed 4 mmol criterion, but that was validated on a specific population and may not apply to your athlete. You eyeball it. You pick a number. You hope it is right.

Then comes the harder question: now what? The test told you where the athlete's thresholds are. But which training block should come next? How much intensity? How many weeks? What sessions? The test-to-prescription gap is where most coaches fall back on intuition, habit, or whatever plan template they used last season.

The tools that could help are either too expensive or too limited. INSCYD costs EUR 49+/month per coach, charges per athlete, and stops at the metabolic profile -- it does not tell you what to train. TrainingPeaks is a calendar, not an analysis tool. Intervals.icu is excellent for power data but has zero lactate support. AI coaching apps generate workouts from a black box and never integrate actual lactate test data.

The result: coaches who care about physiology spend hours on analysis, make prescription decisions based on experience rather than evidence, and cannot systematically verify whether their training is moving the right physiological levers.

### The Gap

The gap is not in testing. Portable lactate analyzers cost under EUR 300. Any coach can run a step test in 20 minutes.

The gap is what happens after the test.

**Test → Numbers → ???**

That question mark is where PeakAerobic lives.

### The Origin

PeakAerobic started when a coach got tired of the gap. Tired of spending 45 minutes in Excel to get a single threshold value he was not sure about. Tired of paying EUR 49/month for a tool that gave him a profile but no prescription. Tired of explaining to athletes why their training plan was based on "experience and judgment" instead of the data they had just paid to collect.

He started building. First, a better threshold detection engine -- not one method, but three, cross-validated with a confidence score so you know when to trust the result. Then, a metabolic profiling layer -- VO2max and VLamax estimates without an expensive lab. Then, the piece no one else had built: an automated periodization engine based on Olbrecht's methodology that looks at the athlete's physiological gaps and selects the right training block.

The principle was simple: if the science exists in peer-reviewed papers, it should be accessible to every coach and athlete, not locked behind a EUR 49/month paywall or buried in a PhD thesis.

### The Vision

**Democratize sports physiology.**

The knowledge exists. Faude, Bishop, Billat, Olbrecht, di Prampero -- decades of research on how to detect thresholds, profile metabolism, and structure training. But that knowledge is trapped in academic papers, expensive software, and the heads of a few expert coaches.

PeakAerobic takes published, peer-reviewed methodology and makes it accessible to any coach or athlete with a lactate test and an internet connection. For free.

### The Mission

**Make evidence-based training accessible to every endurance athlete.**

Not by hiding the science behind a black box. By showing it. Every method cited. Every confidence score explained. Every training recommendation justified by physiological reasoning.

If PeakAerobic recommends a threshold development block, it tells you why: "Your LT2 is 12% below the benchmark for your event, your LT1/LT2 ratio suggests moderate VLamax, and you are 18 weeks from your target race -- threshold development is the priority." You can agree, disagree, or adjust. The tool informs; the coach (or athlete) decides.
